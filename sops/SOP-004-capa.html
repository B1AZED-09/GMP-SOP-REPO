# SOP-004: Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) **Document ID:** SOP-004 **Version:** 1.0 **Effective Date:** 14/10/2026 **Department:** Quality Assurance --- ## 1. PURPOSE To establish a systematic approach for investigating root causes of deviations and implementing actions to prevent recurrence. ## 2. SCOPE All CAPAs arising from deviations, complaints, audit findings, or trend analysis. ## 3. PROCEDURE ### 3.1 CAPA Triggers - Critical or Major deviations (SOP-003) - Customer complaints - Internal/external audit findings - Out-of-trend (OOT) results - Equipment failures ### 3.2 CAPA Process 1. **Problem Description:** Clear, concise statement of the issue 2. **Root Cause Analysis:** Use 5 Whys or Fishbone diagram 3. **Corrective Action:** Action to fix the immediate problem 4. **Preventive Action:** Action to prevent recurrence 5. **Implementation:** Assign responsible person and deadline 6. **Effectiveness Check:** Verify action worked (30-90 days) 7. **Closure:** QA approval ### 3.3 Root Cause Analysis Methods **5 Whys Example:** - Problem: Temperature exceeded 28°C in Room 2 - Why? HVAC system failed - Why? Compressor overheated - Why? Dirty condenser coils - Why? Maintenance schedule not followed - Why? No automated maintenance tracking system - **Root Cause:** Lack of preventive maintenance system ## 4. REFERENCES - ICH Q10: Pharmaceutical Quality System - EU GMP Chapter 1: Quality Management ## 5.
