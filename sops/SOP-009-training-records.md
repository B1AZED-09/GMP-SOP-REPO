# SOP-009: Training Records

**Document ID:** SOP-009  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 14/10/2026  
**Department:** Quality Assurance / Human Resources

---

## 1. PURPOSE

To ensure all personnel are adequately trained for their roles and that training is documented to demonstrate competency.

## 2. SCOPE

All employees, contractors, and temporary staff.

## 3. PROCEDURE

### 3.1 Training Requirements

| Role | Required Training | Frequency |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| All staff | GMP Induction | Before starting work, annual refresher |
| Cultivation Tech | SOP-001 to SOP-010 read and understood | Before independent work |
| QA Staff | Data Integrity (ALCOA+), Deviation Management | Annual |
| Cleanroom staff | Gowning qualification | Semi-annual requalification |

### 3.2 Training Documentation

Each training record must include:
- Employee name and ID
- Training topic/date
- Trainer name
- Assessment method (written test, practical demonstration, or both)
- Pass/fail result
- Employee signature
- Trainer signature

### 3.3 Training Matrix

Maintain a training matrix showing:
- Rows: All employees
- Columns: All required training modules
- Cells: Date completed, expiry date

Review monthly for expired training.
