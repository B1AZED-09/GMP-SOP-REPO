## 1. PURPOSE

To establish a system for identifying, documenting, investigating, and resolving deviations from approved procedures or specifications to ensure product quality and compliance.

## 2. SCOPE

All deviations from Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), batch records, equipment settings, or environmental parameters.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Deviation** | Departure from an approved instruction or established standard |
| **CAPA** | Corrective and Preventive Action |
| **Root Cause** | The fundamental reason for the occurrence of a deviation |
| **Critical Deviation** | Deviation with potential impact on product safety, identity, strength, purity, or quality |

## 4. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Any Employee | Identify and report deviations immediately |
| Supervisor | Assess initial severity, ensure documentation completed |
| QA Manager | Lead investigation, approve CAPA, close deviation |
| Production Manager | Implement corrective actions |

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Deviation Classification

| Class | Definition | Examples | Investigation Timeline |
|-------|------------|----------|----------------------|
| **Critical** | Product quality/safety risk | Contamination, temperature excursion >4 hours, equipment failure during processing | Immediate QA notification, full investigation within 24 hours |
| **Major** | Procedure not followed, no immediate risk to product | Missing signature, late data entry, incorrect cleaning sequence | Investigation within 48 hours |
| **Minor** | Documentation error, corrected immediately | Typo, formatting issue, calculation error caught before approval | Weekly review |

### 5.2 Deviation Process Flow

1. **Identification:** Employee identifies deviation
2. **Immediate Action:** Stop work if critical, notify supervisor
3. **Documentation:** Complete Deviation Report Form within 24 hours
4. **Assessment:** QA determines product impact and batch disposition
5. **Investigation:** Root cause analysis using 5 Whys or Fishbone diagram
6. **CAPA:** Develop and implement corrective/preventive actions
7. **Closure:** QA approval, batch release decision, documentation archive

### 5.3 Deviation Report Form Contents

- Deviation ID (format: DEV-YYYY-####)
- Date and time of occurrence
- Location/area
- Description of deviation (facts only, no opinions)
- Immediate actions taken
- Root cause analysis
- CAPA details with responsible persons and deadlines
- Batch impact assessment (Release/Reject/Rework)
- QA disposition and signature
