# SOP-008: Waste Disposal

**Document ID:** SOP-008  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 14/10/2026  
**Department:** Production / Quality Assurance

---

## 1. PURPOSE

To ensure all waste materials are disposed of safely, legally, and in an environmentally responsible manner while maintaining GMP compliance.

## 2. SCOPE**

All waste generated in cultivation, processing, and laboratory operations.

## 3. WASTE CLASSIFICATION

| Type | Examples | Disposal Method |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **General waste** | Paper, cardboard, packaging | Recycling or general waste bin |
| **Plant waste** | Trim, fan leaves, stalks | Composting or licensed waste contractor |
| **Hazardous chemical** | Pesticides, cleaning chemicals | Licensed hazardous waste disposal |
| **Biohazardous** | Dead plants with disease, used PPE | Autoclave or licensed biohazard disposal |
| **Electronic** | Failed sensors, equipment | WEEE recycling |

## 4. PROCEDURE

### 4.1 General Waste Handling

1. Segregate waste at point of generation
2. Use labeled, color-coded bins:
   - Green: Compostable plant material
   - Yellow: Biohazardous
   - Red: Hazardous chemical
   - Black: General waste
3. Empty bins daily, clean bins weekly

### 4.2 Plant Material Destruction

All cannabis plant material must be:
1. Weighed and recorded in Destruction Log
2. Rendered unusable (shredding or mixing with water/cat litter)
3. Disposed of via licensed waste contractor
4. Documentation retained for 5 years

### 4.3 Documentation

- **Waste Transfer Notes:** Required for all hazardous waste movements
- **Destruction Log:** Date, weight, method, witness signature
- **Training Records:** All staff trained on waste procedures

## 5. REFERENCES

- Health Canada Cannabis Regulations: Section 63 (Waste)
- EU Waste Framework Directive 2008/98/EC
