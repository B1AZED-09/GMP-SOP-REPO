# SOP-006: Equipment Calibration **Document ID:** SOP-006 **Version:** 1.0 **Effective Date:** 14/10/2026 **Department:** Engineering / Quality Assurance --- ## 1. PURPOSE To ensure all equipment used in GMP operations is calibrated against traceable standards to maintain accuracy and reliability. ## 2. SCOPE All measurement and control equipment including temperature, humidity, pressure, pH, EC, and weighing devices. ## 3. PROCEDURE ### 3.1 Calibration Schedule | Equipment Type | Frequency | Standard | Tolerance | |----------------|-----------|----------|-----------| | Temperature sensors | Monthly | NIST-traceable thermometer | ±0.5°C | | RH sensors | Monthly | NIST-traceable hygrometer | ±2% RH | | pH meters | Daily (verify), Monthly (calibrate) | pH 4.0, 7.0, 10.0 buffers | ±0.1 pH | | EC meters | Daily (verify), Monthly (calibrate) | 1.413 mS/cm standard | ±2% | | Scales/Balances | Daily (verify), Quarterly (calibrate) | NIST-traceable weights | ±0.1% | | Pressure gauges | Quarterly | Deadweight tester | ±1% | ### 3.2 Calibration Procedure 1. **Pre-check:** Verify equipment is clean and functional 2. **As-found reading:** Record reading before adjustment 3. **Adjustment:** Adjust to match standard if outside tolerance 4. **As-left reading:** Record reading after adjustment 5. **Documentation:** Complete Calibration Log with date, technician, standards used 6. **Label:** Update calibration sticker with date and next due date ### 3.3 Out-of-Tolerance Handling If equipment fails calibration: 1. Tag equipment "DO NOT USE" 2. Assess impact on batches since last successful calibration 3. Notify QA Manager 4. Repair or replace equipment 5. Document investigation in deviation system (SOP-003) ## 4. REFERENCES - EU GMP Chapter 3: Premises and Equipment - ISO 9001:2015 Section 7.1.5: Monitoring and Measuring Resources ## 5.
