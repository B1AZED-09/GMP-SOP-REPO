## 1. PURPOSE

To establish procedures for monitoring critical environmental parameters in cultivation areas to ensure compliance with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and product quality standards.

## 2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to all cultivation rooms, propagation areas, and post-harvest processing zones within the facility.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **ALCOA+** | Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available |
| **CPP** | Critical Process Parameter - a parameter that must be controlled to ensure product quality |
| **RH** | Relative Humidity (%) |
| **VPD** | Vapor Pressure Deficit (kPa) - measure of the drying capacity of air |
| **Deviation** | Departure from an approved instruction or established standard |

## 4. RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Cultivation Technician | Execute monitoring, record data, report deviations |
| Quality Assurance Assistant | Review daily logs, verify data integrity |
| QA Manager | Approve deviations, assess batch impact |
| Facility Maintenance | Calibrate sensors, maintain HVAC systems |

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Monitoring Schedule

Environmental parameters shall be monitored continuously via automated sensors, with manual verification every 2 hours during operational periods.

| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Alert Threshold | Critical Threshold |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Temperature | 20-26°C | &gt;28°C or &lt;18°C | &gt;30°C or &lt;15°C |
| Relative Humidity | 55-70% | &gt;75% or &lt;50% | &gt;80% or &lt;40% |
| CO2 Concentration | 800-1200 ppm | &gt;1500 ppm | &gt;2000 ppm |
| VPD | 0.9-1.3 kPa | &gt;1.5 kPa | &gt;1.8 kPa |
| Light Intensity | 400-600 μmol/m²/s | &gt;700 or &lt;300 | &gt;800 or &lt;200 |

### 5.2 Data Recording Requirements

All environmental data must be recorded in accordance with ALCOA+ principles:

1. **Attributable:** Every entry must include the technician's initials and timestamp
2. **Legible:** Data must be recorded in standard units (°C, %, ppm, kPa)
3. **Contemporaneous:** Record data at the time of observation, not retrospectively
4. **Original:** Automated sensor data is the original record; manual entries must not overwrite
5. **Accurate:** Sensors must be calibrated monthly against NIST-traceable standards

### 5.3 Deviation Handling

If any parameter exceeds the **Alert Threshold**:

1. **Immediate Action:** Notify the Shift Supervisor within 15 minutes
2. **Documentation:** Complete Deviation Report Form (see SOP-003) within 24 hours
3. **Assessment:** QA must assess impact on affected batches within 48 hours
4. **CAPA:** If root cause is identified, implement Corrective and Preventive Action

If any parameter exceeds the **Critical Threshold**:

1. **Immediate Action:** Stop all processing, isolate affected area
2. **Notification:** Contact QA Manager immediately (phone/email)
3. **Batch Hold:** Place all batches in affected area on hold status
4. **Investigation:** Full root cause analysis required before resuming operations

### 5.4 Equipment Calibration

| Equipment | Calibration Frequency | Standard | Tolerance |
|-----------|---------------------|----------|-----------|
| Temperature Sensors | Monthly | NIST-traceable thermometer | ±0.5°C |
| RH Sensors | Monthly | NIST-traceable hygrometer | ±2% |
| CO2 Sensors | Quarterly | Certified gas mixture | ±50 ppm |
| Light Sensors | Annually | NIST-traceable photometer | ±5% |
