# SOP-002: Cleaning and Sanitization of Cultivation Areas **Document ID:** SOP-002 **Version:** 1.0 **Effective Date:** 01/11/2026 **Review Date:** 01/11/2027 **Author:** [Theo Kerr] **Approved By:** [To be completed by QA Manager] **Department:** Cultivation / Quality Assurance --- ## 1. PURPOSE To establish procedures for cleaning and sanitization of cultivation areas, equipment, and tools to prevent cross-contamination, maintain GMP compliance, and ensure product quality and safety. ## 2. SCOPE This procedure applies to all cultivation rooms, propagation areas, post-harvest processing zones, and associated equipment within the facility. ## 3. DEFINITIONS | Term | Definition | |------|------------| | **ATP Swabbing** | Adenosine Triphosphate bioluminescence testing to measure organic residue | | **HEPA Vacuum** | High-Efficiency Particulate Air filter vacuum for controlled dust removal | | **IPA** | Isopropyl Alcohol (70% v/v solution) used for sanitization | | **RLU** | Relative Light Units - measurement unit for ATP test results | | **CFU** | Colony Forming Units - measurement of microbial contamination | | **WFI** | Water for Injection - pharmaceutical-grade purified water | | **Sporicidal** | Agent capable of killing bacterial spores | ## 4. RESPONSIBILITIES | Role | Responsibility | |------|----------------| | Cultivation Technician | Execute daily cleaning, record data, report issues | | QA Assistant | Perform ATP/microbial swabbing, verify cleaning logs | | QA Manager | Approve validation results, assess cleaning failures | | Facility Maintenance | Maintain HEPA vacuums, supply cleaning agents | ## 5. PROCEDURE ### 5.1 Daily Cleaning (End of Shift) Daily cleaning shall be performed at the end of each shift in all active cultivation areas. #### 5.1.1 Plant Material Removal 1. Remove all dead leaves, stems, and plant debris manually using gloved hands or sterile tweezers 2. Place debris in designated waste bins (refer to SOP-008: Waste Disposal) 3. Do not allow plant material to accumulate on floors or surfaces 4. Complete removal should take no more than 30 minutes per room #### 5.1.2 HEPA Vacuum 1. Use HEPA-filtered vacuum only (standard vacuums are prohibited) 2. Vacuum all floor areas, corners, and under benching in a systematic pattern 3. Vacuum any horizontal surfaces where dust has accumulated 4. Empty vacuum bag/canister into waste bin after use 5. Document vacuum use in Cleaning Log (Appendix 1) with time and technician initials #### 5.1.3 Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) Cleaning 1. Prepare 70% IPA solution in spray bottle (or use pre-made 70% IPA bottles) 2. Spray all high-touch surfaces: - Door handles - Light switches - Thermometer/sensor frames - Watering can handles - Workbenches 3. Wipe with lint-free cloth or disposable wipes in one direction only 4. Allow surface to air dry (do not re-wipe) 5. Contact time: minimum 1 minute before wiping #### 5.1.4 Floor Cleaning 1. Sweep floor to remove loose debris 2. Mop with appropriate disinfectant (see 5.2.2 below) 3. Use designated mop heads for each room (do not cross-contaminate between areas) 4. Allow floor to dry completely before staff re-enter 5. Document in Cleaning Log with time, product used, and technician initials #### 5.1.5 Daily Cleaning Log Completion - Record cleaning date, time, technician initials - Note any damaged equipment or areas requiring repair - Sign and date the log (ALCOA+ compliance) --- ### 5.2 Weekly Deep Clean (Every 7 Days) Conducted on designated day (e.g., Friday evenings) with room closure for 24 hours minimum. #### 5.2.1 Room Preparation 1. Remove all mobile equipment (trays, pots, tools, sensors) 2. Move plants to adjacent cultivation area (if applicable) 3. Power down HVAC system for cleaning (coordinate with Facility Maintenance) 4. Close room to personnel #### 5.2.2 Disinfectant Application 1. Apply sporicidal disinfectant (e.g., Virkon S, 1% solution): - Mix according to manufacturer's instructions using WFI - Use portable pump sprayer or misting system - Cover all surfaces: walls, benching, light frames, ceiling vents 2. Contact time: **10 minutes minimum** 3. Do not allow surface to dry; keep moist throughout contact time #### 5.2.3 HEPA Vacuum and Rinse 1. After contact time, HEPA vacuum all surfaces to remove excess disinfectant 2. Rinse all surfaces with WFI or pharmaceutical-grade water: - Use spray bottle or misting system - Ensure no disinfectant residue remains - Dry with lint-free cloths if necessary 3. Wipe high-touch areas again with 70% IPA #### 5.2.4 Equipment Sanitization 1. All removable equipment (trays, pots, tools) shall be: - Rinsed in WFI to remove soil - Immersed in ultrasonic bath containing 70% IPA for 10 minutes - Air dried on clean paper towels - Stored in designated clean storage area 2. Electrical equipment (sensors, probes) cleaned with IPA-dampened cloth only (no liquid immersion) #### 5.2.5 Post-Cleaning Environmental Monitoring 1. Before re-occupancy, take environmental readings: - Temperature, RH, CO2 (per SOP-001) - Verify parameters are within acceptable range 2. Document readings in Environmental Monitoring Log 3. Approval required from QA before plant re-entry #### 5.2.6 Weekly Deep Clean Log - Document date, time, personnel involved - Record disinfectant used and contact time - Note any repairs completed - Signatures from technician and supervisor --- ### 5.3 Cleaning Validation (Quality Assurance) Cleaning effectiveness is verified through ATP swabbing and microbial monitoring. #### 5.3.1 ATP Swabbing - **Frequency:** Random surfaces tested weekly (minimum 5 surfaces per room) - **Procedure:** 1. Swab 10 cm × 10 cm area using sterile ATP swab 2. Areas to test: benching, floor corners, door handles, light frames 3. Follow ATP device manufacturer's protocol 4. Record result immediately (RLU value) - **Pass Criteria:** <150 RLU (indicates <5 μg of ATP residue) - **Failure Response:** See Section 6 (Deviation Handling) #### 5.3.2 Microbial Monitoring - **Frequency:** Monthly air sampling (or weekly if previous failures) - **Procedure:** 1. Use passive air plates (contact plates) or active air sampler 2. Expose plates for 4 hours in room center 3. Incubate at 35°C for bacterial growth, 20–25°C for fungal growth 4. Count colonies after 5 days (bacteria) or 7 days (fungi) - **Pass Criteria:** <10 CFU/m³ for bacteria, <5 CFU/m³ for fungi - **Documentation:** Store plates in archive; attach to Cleaning Validation Report #### 5.3.3 Surface Sampling - **Frequency:** Monthly (or after equipment repair/replacement) - **Procedure:** 1. Swab equipment contact surfaces (e.g., watering nozzles, trays) 2. Culture swabs for aerobic bacteria and mold/yeast 3. Incubate and identify any contamination - **Action Level:** >100 CFU/swab requires re-cleaning #### 5.3.4 Cleaning Validation Report All validation results compiled monthly by QA Assistant: - ATP results with dates and locations - Microbial plate counts with media type - Any failed samples and re-testing results - Trends or concerning patterns - Approved by QA Manager --- ### 5.4 Cleaning Agent Specifications | Agent | Concentration | Use Case | Contact Time | Disposal | |-------|---------------|----------|--------------|----------| | 70% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) | 70% v/v | High-touch surfaces, equipment | 1 min | Evaporate or waste container | | Sporicidal Disinfectant (e.g., Virkon S) | 1% solution | Weekly deep clean | 10 min | Neutralize per manufacturer, drain | | HEPA Vacuum Filter | N/A | Dust and debris removal | N/A | Replace when clogged; dispose as waste | **Note:** All cleaning agents must be compatible with facility materials and approved for use in food/pharmaceutical environments. --- ### 5.5 Equipment Maintenance (Cleaning Tools) | Equipment | Maintenance | Frequency | |-----------|------------|-----------| | HEPA Vacuum | Empty canister, inspect filter | After each use | | Mop Heads | Launder or replace | Weekly | | Spray Bottles | Rinse with WFI, air dry | Daily | | Ultrasonic Bath | Empty, refill with fresh 70% IPA | Weekly | | Lint-Free Cloths | Launder in clinical-grade detergent | Weekly | --- ## 6. DEVIATION HANDLING ### 6.1 Failed ATP Swab (<150 RLU) 1. **Immediate Action:** Re-clean affected surface using Section 5.1 or 5.2 protocol 2. **Re-test:** ATP swab same area within 24 hours 3. **Documentation:** Complete Deviation Report (SOP-003) 4. **Investigation:** If second swab fails, identify root cause (cleaning procedure error, equipment malfunction) 5. **CAPA:** Implement corrective action (additional training, equipment repair, procedural change) 6. **Batch Impact:** Assess whether affected batches require re-assessment or rework ### 6.2 Elevated Microbial Counts 1. **Alert Level (5–10 CFU/m³):** Increase cleaning frequency to twice weekly; re-sample in 1 week 2. **Critical Level (>10 CFU/m³):** Investigate source (HVAC filter, personnel, water system); initiate root cause analysis 3. **Documentation:** Deviation Report and comprehensive investigation report 4. **Batch Hold:** Place batches from previous 7 days on hold; assess contamination risk 5. **CAPA:** Replace HVAC filters, additional staff training, or equipment replacement ### 6.3 Contamination Found on Equipment 1. If CFU >100 on equipment swab: Remove equipment from service immediately 2. Quarantine equipment, label "Do Not Use" 3. Perform intensive cleaning and re-validation 4. Re-test before returning to service 5. Investigate source of contamination --- ## 7.
